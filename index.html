Sizzy Help & FAQ

Honest answers about sizing mathematics, brand facts, and privacy guarantees.

Where does the brand data come from?

Directly from each brand's publicly published size guides, manual check-ups and official web documentation. Every recommendation links to the live brand source page. We never scrape or guess.

How does Sizzy estimate my chest size?

Sizzy uses anthropometric modeling combining height and BMI with build offsets (-2cm for Slim, +3cm for Athletic, up to +8cm for Bigger builds). If you specify a brand size you already own, it blends 50/50 with that brand's chest midpoint.

What if a size feels slightly off?

Use the "Did it fit?" feedback button in your Fit Journal. Marking "Too tight" or "Too loose" nudges your personal offset for that brand by 1 cm, tailoring all future recommendations.

Are photos ever required?

No. You can add front and back photos in Studio for a quick estimate. They are analysed in your browser and never uploaded or stored.
